Our Story

More Than a
Driving School.

Prashant Driving School was founded in 2009 with one mission: to make every student a confident, safe, and responsible driver. Over 15 years, we've trained thousands of students across Mumbai and built a reputation for quality, patience, and results.

Our team of certified instructors doesn't just teach traffic rules — we build your instincts, your confidence, and your respect for road safety. Whether you're a complete beginner or looking to sharpen your skills, we have a program designed for you.
